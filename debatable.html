Debatable

AI pipeline · debate-show videos

Type in a topic and get back a finished debate-show video. Four AI debaters argue it over several rounds, a judge scores each round and names a winner, and the pipeline renders the whole show with voices, captions and a scoreboard.

The cast

Each debater argues from one angle, taken from the classical modes of persuasion.

Logan · logic.Argues from evidence and reasoning.
Piper · emotion.Argues from how it feels and who it affects.
Echo · ethics.Argues from what's right and who can be trusted.
Kai · timing.Argues from why it matters now.

How it works

Debate. The four debaters take turns over several rounds. Each one carries a simple emotional state (conviction, temper, fatigue) that changes how they speak as the debate goes on.
Judge. A fifth AI scores every round and declares a winner.
Voices. Each line is turned into speech, with a different voice per debater.
Captions. Subtitles are timed to the speech and coloured by speaker.
Render. The show is assembled as a 1920×1080 video with a cold open, animated speakers, the verdict scoreboard and a closing card.
Check. The finished file is checked automatically for length, size and format before it counts as done.

The rebuild

Half the original code was lost in a folder reorganisation, so in July 2026 I rebuilt it from scratch with three changes: renders went from about 20 minutes to under a minute, the robotic voices were replaced with natural ones, and cartoon characters replaced the slow talking-head faces.

Why it's shelved

A product decision, not a technical one. The pipeline works end to end. I built it for fun and found no audience that justified the upkeep of posting every day.
