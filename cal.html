Cal

Web utility · three calendars in one

A personal calendar that shows the Gregorian, Hijri and Gujarati calendars side by side, so one date can be read in all three without a converter.

The idea

Most calendar apps handle one calendar well and treat the others as an afterthought. Cal treats the Gregorian, Hijri and Gujarati calendars as equals in a single view.

For the Hijri calendar, the spec converts dates through a Julian Day Number, a single running count of days that makes moving between calendars reliable. Everything is saved in the browser, with no account.

Where it stands

The spec is finished and the build hasn't started. It's a small, self-contained project I'd like to come back to.
